Support — Vathos Bible

Vathos Bible is a free Bible reading and study app for iPhone and iPad. There is no purchase, no in-app purchase, no ads, and no account to create.

To report a bug or request a feature from inside the app, open You → Send Feedback. It is anonymous: it sends only your message, whether it is a bug or a feature request, and the app and iOS versions, with no name or email, so we can't reply to it. If you'd like an answer, email instead.

For app questions, bugs, or privacy questions, email VathosBible@icloud.com.

Personal notes, highlights, and reading progress stay on your device. If you are signed into iCloud, they sync through your private iCloud database. Creditch does not run a Vathos server. ESV, NIV, NLT, and NKJV are read online from their publishers' services and need a connection; the bundled BSB, KJV, and WEB work offline.
