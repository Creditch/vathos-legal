What it does

A study Bible that shows its sources.

Read

The Berean Standard Bible, KJV and WEB work offline, with many more public-domain translations in English and other languages to download. ESV, NIV, NLT and NKJV read online through their publishers.

Study a verse

Interlinear, lexicon, concordance, cross-references and Compare, from the verse you selected, without leaving the chapter.

Learn Greek and Hebrew

A structured Koine Greek and Biblical Hebrew course that uses the same tagged text you read every day.

Keep your own work

Highlights, notes, bookmarks, a journal and reading plans. They sync through your own iCloud, not a Vathos server.
